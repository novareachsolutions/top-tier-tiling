/How it works

How a wet-area job goes together

In bathrooms and laundries, preparation, waterproofing and tiling need to happen in the right order and work together properly. We treat this as one connected process rather than a series of separate jobs.

01

Surface preparation

Checking the existing surface and preparing it before any screeding, waterproofing or tiling begins.

02

Screeding

Levelling and shaping the floor to get the base and falls right for what comes next.

03

Waterproofing

Applying waterproofing to bathrooms, laundries and other wet areas ahead of tiling.

04

Tiling

Installing ceramic, porcelain, mosaic or natural stone tiles to walls and floors, with proper finishing at edges and corners.

05

Renovation completion

Bringing the tiling and wet-area work together as part of the finished renovation.

/Tiles

Tiles we supply

Tiles supplied, or customer-supplied — whichever suits. We supply ceramic, porcelain, mosaic and natural stone tiles for wall and floor tiling, or we're happy to install tiles you've already bought.

Which option works best depends on the room, the surface, and how the space gets used day to day. It's something we talk through as part of the quote.
