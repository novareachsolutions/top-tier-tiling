About Top Tier Tiling Group

A Melbourne-based tiling and renovation business operating since 2017, with a five-person team covering tiling, waterproofing, screeding, and bathroom, laundry and kitchen renovations.

The business

Top Tier Tiling Group is a sole trader tiling and renovation business based in Melbourne, operating since 2017. The team of five covers tiling, waterproofing, screeding, bathroom renovations, laundry renovations and kitchen renovations for residential properties — individual trade jobs as well as larger renovation projects.

We supply porcelain, ceramic and subway tiles as part of our tiling work, alongside the waterproofing and screeding needed to prepare wet areas properly before the tiles go down.

Our approach

  • Understanding the job before work starts — what's needed, and in what order
  • Preparing surfaces properly, rather than working around shortcuts
  • Completing work to an agreed timeframe
  • Keeping the quality of workmanship consistent throughout the job, not just at the visible finish
  • Clear communication from the first enquiry through to the job being finished
