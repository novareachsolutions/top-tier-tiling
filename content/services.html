Tiling & Renovation Services in Melbourne

From a single re-tiled shower to a full bathroom, laundry or kitchen renovation — tiling, waterproofing and screeding, handled by one Melbourne-based team since 2017.

Trade services

The individual services behind most renovation jobs. Each can be booked on its own, or combined with the others depending on what the job needs.

Tiling

Wall and floor tiling using porcelain tiles, ceramic tiles and subway tiles, supplied and installed for bathrooms, kitchens, laundries and living areas.

See Tiling

Waterproofing

Waterproofing services for wet areas, applied before tiling to help protect the underlying structure from water penetration.

See Waterproofing

Screeding

Screeding to prepare and level floors, creating a suitable base for waterproofing membranes and tile installation.

See Screeding

Renovations

Renovation jobs that bring a few of these services together — screeding, waterproofing and tiling handled as one coordinated stage of the job, rather than separate trades to manage yourself.

Bathroom Renovations

Screeding, waterproofing and tiling coordinated as one stage of a bathroom renovation.

See Bathroom Renovations

Laundry Renovations

Laundry renovation work for a practical, water-resistant finish, from floor preparation through to the final tiled surface.

See Laundry Renovations

Kitchen Renovations

Kitchen renovation and tiling work, including splashback tiling and floor tiling as part of a broader kitchen renovation project.

See Kitchen Renovations

Not sure which service you need?

Most renovation jobs combine two or three of these services — a bathroom renovation, for example, typically needs screeding, waterproofing and tiling together. Get in touch with a description of the space and what you have in mind, and we can work out the right combination from there.
